Our Story

A pursuit of
the exceptional

Victoria Stone Gallery was established in 2017 by a team whose decades of experience in the global stone trade run from fabrication workshops in New Zealand to the processing factories and stone-producing regions of Brazil — a country one of us calls home by birth and another by nearly a decade of choice. Our network extends further still: we travel regularly and maintain inspectors and longstanding relationships with factories and suppliers across Italy, Turkey, India and beyond. These are not transactional connections. They are partnerships built over years, in person, on the factory floor. That proximity to the source is not incidental. It is the foundation of everything we offer.

Today, our 2000m² gallery in Melbourne's south-east stands as one of Australia's most significant natural stone destinations. Every stone on display has been carefully selected and inspected at the source before travelling to Australia.

Gallery Visits & Events

Experience Stone
in Person

We regularly host architects, interior designers, stonemasons and developers for guided gallery evenings — an opportunity to understand stone beyond the surface. Origin, geology, formation, fabrication considerations and material behaviour: conversations that inform better design decisions. Accompanied, of course, by good food and wine.
